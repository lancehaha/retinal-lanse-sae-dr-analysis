## Current scientific boundary

The completed feature atlas identifies prominent SAE activation patterns and records DR/non-DR statistics as separate audit fields. A bounded local language-grounding pilot and matching 1,024-image VLM ablations are complete, and the label-free global reconstruction experiment has passed its frozen internal-test audit. Experiment 11 is testing a new high-resolution tile representation because the prior global and 224px patch dictionaries had no mask-grounded lesion mapping. No SAE feature has a clinically validated meaning. Blinded ophthalmologist adjudication, clinician-reviewed external evaluation, and an approved labeled external test remain required.
