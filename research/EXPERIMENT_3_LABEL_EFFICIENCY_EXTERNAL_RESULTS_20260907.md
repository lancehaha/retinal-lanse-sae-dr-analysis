## Cohorts and execution

| Cohort | Images | Patient/exam units |
|---|---:|---:|
| EyePACS training | 28,130 | 14,065 patients |
| EyePACS validation | 3,476 | 1,738 patients |
| EyePACS locked internal test | 3,518 | 1,759 patients |
| IDRiD clean official testing | 102 | 102 image-examination proxy units |

The IDRiD set started at 103 official testing images. One exact duplicate of an official training image with conflicting grade was excluded; the original archives and raw manifest were preserved. All six label budgets (1%, 5%, 10%, 25%, 50%, 100%) used five deterministic patient-level replicates. All 120 replicate-arm records contained all five training grades, so no five-class record was unavailable.

## Secondary observations

At 100% labels, five-grade locked-test metrics were:

| Arm | Macro-AUROC | Macro-AUPRC | QWK | Macro-F1 | Ordinal MAE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Dense | 0.7524 | 0.4015 | 0.4270 | 0.3716 | 0.7342 |
| Dense+SAE | 0.7541 | 0.3972 | 0.4226 | 0.3795 | 0.7297 |
| SAE reconstruction | 0.7071 | 0.3673 | 0.3541 | 0.3252 | 0.8243 |
| Compact SAE (64) | 0.7004 | 0.3453 | 0.3069 | 0.2529 | 0.9633 |

The compact SAE and reconstruction arms were consistently below dense RETFound on the internal test. Fusion was effectively neutral, with no label-budget regime showing a statistically supported improvement. This supports the bounded conclusion that this frozen SAE does not improve the current downstream DR task’s full-data performance or label efficiency. It does not test lesion semantics, specialist agreement, or patch-level grounding.
