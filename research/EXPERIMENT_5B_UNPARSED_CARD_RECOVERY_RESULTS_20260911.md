## Objective

The original Experiment 5B run had 113 of 256 feature cards whose prior
hypothesis response was marked `unparsed`. This bounded recovery reran those
cards using the prespecified contrastive procedure: original 32 high-activation
discovery images plus a new 32-image low-activation group to generate a
hypothesis, then fresh 32-high/32-low validation. A valid initial failure could
receive one revision and one fresh revalidation; no unlimited retries were
allowed.

## Recovery results

|---|---:|
| Cards requested | 113 |
| Cards processed | 113 |

| One revision attempted | 14 |

| Final failed or unavailable | 2 |
| Hypothesis-generation unavailable | 0 |
| Maximum revisions per card | 1 |

The two final failures were feature IDs 3322 and 7388. Both had parseable
hypotheses and complete initial/revalidation responses, but neither met the
predeclared high-rate and contrast thresholds.

For the 113 recovery cards, the initial validation mean high pattern-present
rate was 0.861, the low-control rate was 0.123, and the mean contrast was 0.738.
For the 14 revalidation rounds, the corresponding means were 0.929, 0.063, and
0.866. These are descriptive same-model VLM judgments, not independent clinical
measurements.

## Interpretation

This completes the bounded recovery and makes all 256 feature cards auditable.
It does not show that any feature is a DR-specific lesion or biologically
meaningful. Hypotheses and judgments were generated by the same local Qwen
model, so the high pass rate is a model-consistency result. Independent
ophthalmologist review and lesion-grounded validation remain necessary.
